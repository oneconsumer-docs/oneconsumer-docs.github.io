## Tips for getting the most out of OneStep

### 1. Be specific about your situation

**Better:** "I earn ₹40,000, rent is ₹18,000, should I get a credit card?"

**Less helpful:** "Should I get a credit card?"

**Why?** The more context you give, the more personalized the advice.

---

### 2. Ask follow-up questions

**Don't just take the first answer. Dig deeper:**

- "Why that option?"
- "What if I can't pay it off?"
- "What are the alternatives?"
- "Can you explain this part more?"

**OneStep is here for the conversation, not just one answer.**

---

### 3. Be honest about your constraints

**If you're struggling financially, say so.**
**If you don't understand something, admit it.**
**If you're scared of making a mistake, share that.**

**OneStep adjusts advice based on where you actually are, not where you "should" be.**

---

### 4. Use OneStep before making big decisions

**Don't decide then ask. Ask then decide.**

OneStep can help you think through options before you commit to anything.
