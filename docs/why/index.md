## The problem with financial advice in India

### It's designed to confuse you

Complex jargon. Hidden fees. Fine print that needs a lawyer to decode. The financial industry profits from confusion.

**OneConsumer translates.** We explain things in plain language. If you've ever felt too embarrassed to ask "what even is HRA?" — that's exactly why we exist.

### It's a sales pitch in disguise

Most "comparison" sites show you whatever pays them the most commission. Most "advice" articles are just ads.

**OneConsumer is transparent.** We tell you when we earn from a recommendation. We show you what actually fits your situation, not what pays us more.

### It's not personalized

Generic advice like "save 20% of your salary" doesn't help when you're earning ₹30,000 and rent is ₹15,000.

**OneConsumer gets personal.** OneStep AI learns about YOUR salary, YOUR city, YOUR goals. Then gives you advice that actually makes sense for your life.

### 1. We educate first, sell second

### 2. We're built for your moment

No jargon. No complicated financial terms. Just straight talk.
