**We educate first. Sell second.**

Most financial sites do it backwards. They show you products first, explain later (if at all).

OneConsumer is built on a different belief: **people make better decisions when they actually understand their options.**

So we teach you first. Help you understand what you're deciding between. THEN help you choose.

## What makes us different

### 1. We're transparent about how we make money

**Most sites:** Hide referral links. Pretend to be "unbiased" while pushing products that pay them the most.

**OneConsumer:** We tell you when we earn from a recommendation. We show you what fits your situation first, not what pays us more.

**Why it matters:** You can trust that our recommendations are actually about you, not our commission.

---

### 2. We personalize everything

**Most sites:** Generic "best credit cards for 2026" lists that don't account for YOUR salary, YOUR city, YOUR spending habits.

**OneConsumer:** OneStep AI learns about you. Your income. Your expenses. Your goals. Then gives you advice tailored to your actual situation.

**Why it matters:** Advice that works for someone earning ₹1 lakh in Mumbai won't work for someone earning ₹40,000 in Pune. We get that.

---

### 3. We speak your language

**Most sites:** Jargon-heavy "expert" language designed to make you feel like you need professional help (which they'll happily sell you).

**OneConsumer:** Plain language. Like texting a friend. No confusing financial terms unless we explain them first.

**Why it matters:** You shouldn't need a finance degree to understand your own money.

---

### 4. We don't spam you

**Most sites:** Sell your phone number to loan companies. You get calls for weeks after one visit.

**OneConsumer:** We don't sell your data. We don't share your number with third parties. You get recommendations, not spam calls.

**Why it matters:** You came for help, not harassment.

---

### 5. We're built for YOUR moment

**Most sites:** Designed for people who already understand finance and are just comparing options.

**OneConsumer:** Built for first-time earners. First job. First card. First loan. The moments when money gets real and you need someone to help you figure it out.

**Why it matters:** You're not behind. You're just starting. We get that.

## What we're NOT

### We're not a bank

We don't issue credit cards or loans. We help you understand them and choose the right one.

### We're not financial advisors (legally speaking)

We provide information and guidance. For complex investments or tax planning, consult a certified financial advisor.

### We're not "unbiased"

No one is. We earn when you sign up for products through us. But we're **transparent** about it, and we prioritize what's right for you.

### We're not perfect

We're a startup. We're learning. We'll make mistakes. But we'll always be honest with you.
