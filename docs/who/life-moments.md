## When NOT to use OneConsumer

### ❌ When you need advanced investment strategies

OneConsumer focuses on basics. For complex portfolio management or tax-optimized investing, talk to a certified financial advisor.

### ❌ When you need immediate loan approval

We help you understand and compare. We don't process loans. For that, you go directly to banks or NBFCs.

### ❌ When you're looking for get-rich-quick schemes

OneConsumer is about making better financial decisions over time. Not shortcuts or "hacks."
