## This might NOT be for you if...

### ❌ You're looking for advanced investment advice

We focus on basics: credit cards, loans, budgeting, first-time financial decisions. For complex investment strategies or wealth management, you'll want a certified financial advisor.

### ❌ You already have a financial advisor

If you're working with someone and it's going well, great! Keep doing that.

### ❌ You're looking for business or corporate finance help

OneConsumer is for personal finance decisions, not business loans or startup funding.

## Why we focus on young Indians

### Because this is when financial habits form

What you do with your first salary sets the pattern for the rest. Starting with good habits (budgeting, saving, asking questions) makes everything easier later.

### Because you're underserved

Banks want customers with high salaries and existing credit history. You're just starting, so you get ignored or pushed toward predatory products.

**OneConsumer meets you where you are.**

### Because you're digitally native

You're comfortable learning online. Asking an AI for help. Consuming content on your phone.

**OneConsumer is built for how you actually live.**

### Because you need straight talk, not lectures

You don't want to be talked down to. You want someone who gets it and explains clearly.

**That's OneConsumer's tone. That's our approach.**
