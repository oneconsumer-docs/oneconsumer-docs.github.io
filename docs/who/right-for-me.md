- How much to save vs spend

- What to do with your first salary

### ✅ You want to learn, not just be sold to

You don't want someone pushing products. You want to understand your options first.

## OneConsumer might NOT be right for you if...

### ❌ You're looking for complex investment advice

We focus on basics: credit cards, loans, budgeting, first-time financial decisions.

**For advanced investment strategies, tax optimization, or wealth management**, you'll want a certified financial advisor.

---

### ❌ You already have strong financial habits

If you've got a budget that works, savings on track, and understand all your financial products — you might not need us.

**OneConsumer is built for people just starting out**, not those who've already figured it out.

---

### ❌ You prefer face-to-face advice

OneConsumer is digital. OneStep is an AI coach. Our content is online.

**If you need in-person guidance**, look for a local financial advisor.

---

### ❌ You're looking for business/corporate finance help

OneConsumer focuses on personal finance. Not business loans, startup funding, or corporate financial planning.
