## Before you move: What to expect

### 💰 First month will be expensive

**One-time costs you need to budget for:**

**Rent setup:**
- Security deposit (2-3 months rent)
- Brokerage (1 month rent)
- First month rent advance
- **Example:** ₹20,000 rent = ₹80,000-1 lakh upfront

**Setup costs:**
- Basic furniture (bed, table, chair)
- Kitchen essentials
- Deposit for gas, electricity
- Internet connection
- **Estimate:** ₹15,000-30,000

**Travel & transition:**
- Moving your stuff
- Hotels/PG during search
- Local travel during house hunting
- **Estimate:** ₹5,000-15,000

**Total first month:** ₹1-1.5 lakhs easily

## City-specific cost realities

**Rent:** ₹12,000-25,000 (sharing) | ₹18,000-40,000 (1BHK)
**Food:** ₹8,000-12,000/month

## Your moving budget plan

### Before moving: Save up

**Ideal scenario:**
₹1-1.5 lakhs saved before moving

**Realistic scenario:**
₹50,000-70,000 minimum

**If you don't have this:** Negotiate salary advance or borrow from family. First month costs are real.

---

### Month 1: Survival mode

**Focus:** Get settled. Don't worry about savings yet.

**Must-haves:**
- Deposit + rent + brokerage
- Basic setup
- Food and transport

**Nice-to-haves can wait:** New furniture, eating out, entertainment

---

### Month 2-3: Adjust budget

**Once settled, figure out actual costs:**
- What's your real rent?
- Actual food costs?
- Transport (office + personal)?

## Financial adjustments for new city

### 🏠 Rent will be higher

**Old city:** ₹8,000 rent
**New city:** ₹18,000 rent

**That's ₹10,000 less for everything else.**

**Adjust savings expectations.** If you saved ₹8,000 before, maybe ₹3,000 now. That's okay. City transition takes time.

---

### 🍕 Food costs will surprise you

**Eating out is expensive in metros.**

₹150 meal becomes ₹250-300.
₹40 chai becomes ₹80.

**Cook more. Eat out selectively.** At least for first 3-6 months.

---

### 🚗 Transport adds up

**Auto/cab for everything = ₹5,000-7,000/month easily.**

**Better:** Mix of public transport + occasional cab. Budget ₹3,000-4,000.

---

### 💡 Utilities you didn't pay before

**PG → Own place = New costs:**
- Electricity (₹800-1,500/month)
- Water (₹200-400)
- Internet (₹500-800)
- Gas (₹900 refill every 2-3 months)
- Maintenance (if apartment)

**Budget ₹2,000-3,000 extra for utilities.**

### Month 1: Settle in
- ✅ Find place, pay deposits

- ✅ Track everything you spend

- ✅ Increase savings gradually

## Money mistakes to avoid when moving

### ❌ Underestimating first month costs

**Don't:** Move with just ₹30,000 saved for Mumbai.

**Do:** Save ₹80,000-1 lakh. First month is expensive.

---

### ❌ Taking expensive rent you can't sustain

**Don't:** Take ₹25,000 rent on ₹45,000 salary because "it's perfect."

**Do:** Keep rent under 40% of salary. ₹18,000 max in this case.

---

### ❌ Living like you're still in your hometown

**Don't:** Eat out like you did in Tier-2 city. Costs are 2-3x here.

**Do:** Adjust lifestyle for first 6 months. Cook more. Find free entertainment.

---

### ❌ Not having emergency buffer

**Don't:** Spend everything on setup. Live paycheck to paycheck.

**Do:** Keep ₹10,000-15,000 as emergency buffer. Medical, unexpected costs happen.
