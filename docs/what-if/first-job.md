## What to do in your first month

### Week 1: Understand your offer and salary slip

**Before your first salary hits:**
- Understand CTC vs in-hand salary
- Know your deductions (PF, tax, professional tax)
- Clarify HRA, DA, and other allowances
- Set up salary account if needed

### Week 2-3: Track your actual expenses

**Don't budget yet. Just observe:**
- What's your rent?
- Food costs (groceries + eating out)
- Transport (metro, cab, fuel)
- Bills (phone, internet)
- Other regular expenses

**Why?** You can't budget until you know what you actually spend.

---

### Week 4: Make a simple plan

**Once salary hits and you know expenses:**

**Cover basics first:**
- Rent, food, transport, bills = Non-negotiable

**Save something:**
- Even ₹2,000-3,000 = Start the habit

**Keep some for life:**
- Movies, meals out, fun = You earned it

## Financial priorities for first 6 months

### Priority 1: Stabilize your budget

**Goal:** Cover basics, save consistently (even if small).

Don't worry about:
- Maximizing savings
- Optimal investments
- Perfect budget

**Just get stable first.**

---

### Priority 2: Build emergency fund starter

**Goal:** Save ₹10,000-20,000 over 3-4 months.

**Why?** So small emergencies don't derail you.

**Where?** Savings account. Keep it simple and accessible.

---

### Priority 3: Understand credit

## Common first-job financial mistakes (and how to avoid them)

### ❌ Mistake 1: Lifestyle inflation

**What happens:**
First salary feels like a lot. You upgrade everything. Rent. Eating out. Shopping.

By month 3, you're broke and wondering where the money went.

**Better approach:**
Live below your means for 6 months. Build savings cushion. Then slowly upgrade what matters.

---

### ❌ Mistake 2: No emergency fund

**What happens:**
Phone breaks. Medical emergency. Unexpected expense.

No savings. You borrow from friends or take high-interest loans.

**Better approach:**
Build ₹15,000-20,000 buffer in first 3-6 months. It saves you later.

---

### ❌ Mistake 3: Credit card without discipline

**What happens:**
Get credit card. Swipe freely. Can't pay full bill. Interest piles up.

**Better approach:**
Only get credit card when you can commit to paying full bill monthly. Start with low limit. Build the habit first.

---

### ❌ Mistake 4: Ignoring taxes

**What happens:**
Don't track tax-saving investments. End up with huge tax bill in March.

**Better approach:**
Understand basic tax deductions (80C, HRA). Plan through the year, not last minute.
