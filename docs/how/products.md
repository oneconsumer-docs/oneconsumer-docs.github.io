## What we consider before recommending

### ✅ Your income level
Products must fit what you can realistically afford.

### ✅ Your expenses
Credit limit and EMI must leave breathing room in your budget.

### ✅ Your city
Cost of living affects how much financial headroom you have.

### ✅ Your goals
Why you need it determines what features matter.

### ✅ Your credit history
First-time users need different products than someone with established credit.

### ✅ Your risk comfort
Some people want maximum limit. Some want low limit for discipline. We factor this in.

**Every recommendation considers your full situation.**
