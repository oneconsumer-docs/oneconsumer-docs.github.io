## The three parts of OneConsumer

### 1. 💬 OneStep AI - Your personal financial buddy

**What it is:** An AI-powered coach that chats with you, learns about your situation, and gives you personalized advice.

**When to use it:** Anytime you have a question or need to make a decision.

"Should I get a credit card?"
"How much should I save from my ₹40,000 salary?"
"Is this loan a good deal?"

**What makes it special:** It's personalized to YOU. Your salary. Your city. Your goals. Not generic advice.

[Learn more about OneStep AI →]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/how/onestep/)

### 2. 📚 Content & Guides - Learn at your own pace

**What it is:** Clear, jargon-free guides on credit cards, loans, budgeting, and all the financial decisions you're facing.

**When to use it:** When you want to understand something before making a decision.

Browse topics like:
- "What to do with your first salary"
- "How credit cards actually work"

**What makes it special:** Written like we're texting a friend. No corporate speak. Just straight talk.

**What it is:** Smart recommendations for credit cards and loans based on your actual situation.

**When to use it:** When you're ready to actually get a product (after you understand your options).

## How OneStep AI personalizes advice

**OneStep asks about:**
- Your salary and take-home income
- Your monthly expenses (rent, bills, food)
- Your city (costs vary!)
- Your financial goals
- Your current situation

**Then it tailors every answer to YOUR reality.**

**Not:**
_"Credit cards are useful for building credit."_

**OneStep:**
_"Based on your ₹35,000 salary in Pune, a zero-fee card with low limit makes sense. Pay it off monthly to build credit history without risk."_

**That's the difference.**

## The OneConsumer flow (in practice)

### Example: Getting your first credit card

**Week 1:** You ask OneStep "Should I get a credit card?"
- OneStep asks about your salary, expenses, goals
- Explains how credit cards work
- Helps you understand if you're ready

**Week 2:** You read our credit card guide
- Learn about interest rates, billing cycles, fees
- Understand how to use cards responsibly
- See what to watch out for

**Week 3:** You're ready to get one
- OneStep shows you 2-3 cards that fit your ₹40k salary
- Explains why each one makes sense for you
- You pick the one that feels right

**Month 1-12:** You use it responsibly
- OneStep reminds you about payment dates
- Helps you track spending
- Answers questions as they come up

**That's how it works. Education → Understanding → Decision → Support.**
