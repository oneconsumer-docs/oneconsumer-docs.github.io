## About OneConsumer

### What is OneConsumer?

OneConsumer is a financial guidance platform for young Indians making their first big financial decisions.

We combine:
- **OneStep AI:** Personalized financial coaching
- **Educational content:** Clear guides on credit cards, loans, budgeting
- **Product matching:** Smart recommendations for your situation

**Our mission:** Help Indians make better financial decisions.

**Built for:**

**Especially helpful if:**

## About OneStep AI

### What is OneStep AI?

OneStep is an AI-powered financial coach that chats with you, learns about your situation, and gives personalized advice.

**Think of it as:** A financially-smart friend available 24/7 who never judges.

**What makes it different:** It's personalized to YOUR salary, expenses, city, and goals. Not generic advice.

[Learn more about OneStep →]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/how/onestep/)

---

### How does OneStep personalize advice?

OneStep asks about:
- Your monthly salary and expenses
- Your city (cost of living matters)
- Your financial goals
- Your current situation

**Then tailors every answer to your reality.**

**Example:**
Generic advice: "Save 50% of salary"
OneStep: "With your ₹40k salary and ₹18k rent, save ₹5,000 (12%). That's realistic for you."

[How personalization works →]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/how/personalized/)

---

### Can I trust OneStep's advice?

**OneStep is AI-powered, which means:**
✅ It learns from extensive financial knowledge
✅ It asks clarifying questions to understand your situation
✅ It gives advice tailored to your specific circumstances

**But remember:**
- OneStep gives guidance, not guarantees
- For complex situations, consult a certified financial advisor

**Try it. See if the advice helps. Then decide if you trust it.**

## Pricing & Business Model

### Is OneConsumer free?

**Yes. OneStep AI is currently in beta and free to use.**

No subscription. No hidden fees. No credit card required.

---

### How does OneConsumer make money?

**We earn from product referrals.**

When you sign up for a credit card or loan through OneConsumer, we earn a referral fee from the financial institution.

**We're transparent about this.** When we recommend a product, we tell you we earn from it.

---

### Are your recommendations biased because you earn from them?

**Fair question. Here's how we handle it:**

**Our approach:**
1. **Understand your situation first:** Before recommending anything
2. **Show what fits:** Not what pays us the most
3. **Be transparent:** We tell you when we earn from a recommendation
4. **Sometimes say no:** We tell you when you shouldn't get a product, even if we'd earn from it

**Our belief:** Helping you make good decisions builds trust. Trust builds long-term relationship. That's more valuable than one-time commission.

---

### Do I have to buy products through you?

**No.**

You can:
- Use OneStep for advice only
- Take recommendations elsewhere
- Just learn and make your own decisions

**We're here to help. Not to force sales.**
